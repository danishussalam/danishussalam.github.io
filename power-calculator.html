FAQ

        
Common questions

        

          

            How accurate are the numbers? +
            
They use the published formulas listed under Methods, with t-distribution critical values for small samples, and were checked against G*Power, Stata's power commands, independent recalculations and simulations. Designs marked "Approximation" rely on simplifying assumptions that are explained on the page; treat them as planning guides.

          

          

            Does the AI calculate my sample size? +
            
No. The assistant explains concepts, helps you choose assumptions and suggests calculator settings. Every number comes from the calculator, and settings only change when you press "Apply to calculator".

          

          

            What happens to my data? +
            
Calculations run in your browser. Your conversation and settings are saved only in your own browser so you can return to them. Chat messages are sent to the AI provider to generate replies and are not stored on this site's server. Avoid sharing confidential information in the chat.

          

          

            How do I cite this tool? +
            
Cite the methodological source shown for your design (it is included in the pre-registration paragraph), and optionally: Us-Salam, D. (2026). Power Calculator. https://danishussalam.github.io/power-calculator.html

          

          

            What are the main limitations? +
            
Power depends on assumptions you supply, especially the effect size, intra-cluster correlation and autocorrelation, so use pilot or baseline data where possible and check the sensitivity table. Complex designs (for example staggered adoption with heterogeneous effects) may need simulation-based power analysis.

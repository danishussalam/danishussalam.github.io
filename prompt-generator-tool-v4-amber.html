Your questions, answered

What is prompt engineering?

Prompt engineering is the practice of crafting clear, structured inputs to get better outputs from AI tools. A well-built prompt gives the AI a role, context, and constraints — reducing vague or off-target responses.

How is this different from just typing into ChatGPT directly?

Unstructured prompts produce inconsistent results. Frameworks add discipline: they ensure your prompt includes the right role, context, and output format. The result is more focused, useful AI responses — first time, every time.

Which framework should I choose?

It depends on your task. Use CO-STAR when tone and audience matter. Use Few-Shot when you want outputs that match a specific style. Use CoT for logical or step-by-step reasoning. The Hybrid option intelligently combines frameworks — a good default if you're unsure.

Can I use the outputs for professional or published work?

Yes, with care. This tool generates prompt templates — what you do with the AI outputs is your responsibility. Always review AI responses critically and follow any relevant policies at your organisation.

Is my input stored or shared?

Your input is sent to an AI model to generate a refined prompt. We track only a simple counter of total uses — we do not log, store, or analyse the content of what you enter. Avoid entering sensitive or confidential information.

What if the output doesn't look right?

Refine your input. Be more specific about your goal, audience, or constraints, then regenerate. You can also switch frameworks — some work better for certain tasks. The Hybrid option is a good fallback if a single framework isn't working.
